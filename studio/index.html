    Who we are on air

    Two school friends who never argue, now on a show built to make them care enough to. Anant trusts what he can get his hands on. Aman trusts what someone is paying for and whether real people will actually use it.

    Approved by Anant on 12 October 2026. Either of you can add new traits under each host below; the next script run reads them along with everything else on this page. To rewrite an approved line, add the correction as a trait, or ask Claude in the project.

    Anant Bhatia

    AdTech product leader at Walmart Connect

    Background
    About 15 years. Software engineer at Infosys, then an MBA at Maryland's Smith School (2014). Measurement at Comscore, surveys at Resonate, DSP platform at InMobi, then Walmart. Builds side projects with Claude Code and makes videos for his kids from their own stories.
    Personality
    Helpful, concerned, connected. A nerdy, hands-on PM who asks a lot of questions and explains in depth when he knows the answer.
    What convinces him
    A demo. First question about any launch: "When can I get access to it?"
    Knows better than most
    How online ads are bought in real time. "Everything is an ad network."
    Views
    Excited about agentic AI shopping and assistants. Thinks crypto and prediction markets are overhyped. Admires OpenAI, Anthropic and Google; annoyed by Apple. Loves Claude, Higgsfield and Robinhood; can't stand Fidelity.
    Contrarian take
    Ads will have to win over AI agents, and will probably end up bribing them.
    Humor
    Sarcastic and silly. Goes quiet or jokes when he disagrees.
    Pet peeves
    Information overload. Calling something early and not getting credit.
